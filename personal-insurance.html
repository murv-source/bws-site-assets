Common questions

Personal & farm coverage questions, answered.

What is a declaration page and why do you need it?

It is the summary page of your current policy showing your coverages, limits, and deductibles. It lets us compare accurately instead of guessing, so the quotes you get back are real.

What does the review cost?

Nothing. The review and the quotes are free with no obligation to buy. You only move forward if the coverage and pricing make sense for you.

Do I have to switch from my current agent?

No. Plenty of people just want a second look at what they are carrying and paying. We are happy to review it, and you decide what to do from there.

Can I bundle personal and farm coverage?

Yes. Home, auto, farm, and the other lines can be quoted together, which is often where the savings and the coverage gaps show up.

Do I need flood coverage if I am not in a flood zone?

Often, yes. Standard home policies exclude flood entirely, and a meaningful share of flood claims come from outside mapped high-risk zones. Your advisor will walk through your specific situation.

How long does it take?

Most reviews come back within a few business days of receiving your declaration page, depending on how many lines you are having quoted.
