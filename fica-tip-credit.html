Common questions

FICA Tip Credit questions, answered.

What is the FICA Tip Credit?

It is a federal tax credit (IRC §45B) for the employer share of Social Security and Medicare tax paid on employee tips. It is a dollar-for-dollar credit against income tax, not a deduction, and eligible businesses may claim it each year they meet the requirements.

Which businesses qualify?

Food and beverage businesses such as restaurants, bars and cafes, plus beauty services such as salons beginning in 2025. Tips must be reported by employees, and the employer share of FICA taxes must be paid. Mandatory service charges do not qualify.

Which tips count toward the credit?

When tipped staff earn at least $5.15/hr (food and beverage) or $7.25/hr (beauty) in cash wages, all reported tips can count toward the credit.

Can my business claim prior years?

Eligible businesses may also qualify for prior-year credits. A review of your payroll records shows which years may apply.

What if my business doesn't owe enough income tax to use the full credit?

The credit is nonrefundable, but unused credit can carry forward to future years.

How much does it cost?

Finding out costs nothing. If a credit is secured, the fee is a flat 20% of the credit. No credit, no fee.

Does this replace my CPA?

No. We review your payroll records, calculate creditable tips and the estimated credit, have an independent CPA check the results, and prepare a draft Form 8846 for your own CPA to file.
