Common questions

Business data monetization questions, answered.

What is Business Data Monetization?

It's the process of licensing eligible business data to companies that use real-world information to build and improve artificial intelligence. We help you find out whether your data may have value, prepare it properly, and guide you through any offers you choose to consider.

What types of business data may have value?

Common examples include CRM records, transaction and sales histories, operational and workflow data, project management records, industry-specific datasets, business process documentation, and historical business records. All data must meet eligibility, ownership, privacy, and licensing requirements.

Do I lose ownership of my data?

No. Licensing is not selling. You keep ownership and control of your data, and any license follows terms you review and approve before anything moves forward.

How is sensitive information protected?

Privacy is reviewed before any data is shared. Eligible information is prepared in line with applicable privacy and security requirements, which can include removing or de-identifying personal information, and is handled through secure processes.

Is there any upfront cost?

No. There are no upfront fees to have your business and data evaluated.

How much could my business earn?

It depends on the type, volume, quality, and history of your data, as well as current buyer demand. We can't promise a specific amount, and not every business will receive an offer. The evaluation is designed to give you a realistic picture before you commit to anything.

How long does the process take?

Timelines vary with the size and condition of your data and how quickly buyers respond. Your initial evaluation is the first step, and we'll keep you informed at every stage after that.

Am I required to accept an offer?

No. You review every offer and its licensing terms, and you decide whether to proceed. There is no obligation to accept.
