Common questions

Do I have to take all eight services?

No. Start with one service or take the whole box. Most businesses begin with the one or two areas costing them the most, then add more as it makes sense.

It depends on which services you take and the size of your team. The review is free, and you see exactly what each service costs, and what bundling saves you, before you commit to anything.

Do I have to switch everything at once?

No. We move services one at a time on a schedule that works for you, and we manage the setup, migration, and enrollment so your operation keeps running.

Who actually delivers each service?

Each service is delivered by specialists in that area and managed through Big Wolf Solutions. You keep one point of contact and one invoice no matter how many services you take.

What size business is this built for?

It scales from small teams to a few thousand employees. The free consultation will confirm exactly how the numbers work for your headcount.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just a short conversation. If you move forward, we will ask for basic information on what you run today so we can show you an accurate comparison.
